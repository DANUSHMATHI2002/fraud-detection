Key findings

  • Fraud clusters in overnight online transactions and in electronics and travel spending.
  • At the cost-optimal threshold the model catches about a third of fraud cases with roughly 19 alerts per 1,000 transactions.
  • Recall in Wales is lower than elsewhere, but only 21 fraud cases sit behind it, so it needs more data before any conclusion.
  • Logistic regression, random forest and XGBoost perform similarly, so a simpler model may be easier to defend in a regulated setting.
